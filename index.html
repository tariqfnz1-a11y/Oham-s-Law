3. Observe the Mathematical Relationship

Ohm's Law

I = V ÷ R

Current = 12 ÷ 6 = 2 A

Electrical power = V × I = 24 W

What is happening?

The circuit has a voltage of 12 V and resistance of 6 Ω. The current is 2 A.

5. Real-Life Connection: Water Flow

Think of electricity as water moving through a pipe:

  • Voltage: Water pressure pushing water through a pipe.
  • Current: The amount of water flowing per second.
  • Resistance: A narrow pipe or obstruction restricting flow.

More pressure can increase flow. A narrower pipe can reduce flow when the pressure remains unchanged.
